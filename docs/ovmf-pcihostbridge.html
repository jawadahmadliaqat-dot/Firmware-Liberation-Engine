Resolved: the old FAIL was QEMU fw_cfg, not a violation

At v3.13 this driver FAILED confinement on exactly three ports: 0x510, 0x514, and 0x518. Port 0x510 is the QEMU fw_cfg selector, written by FUN_0000185b (OUT at 000018bf, reached by a tail jump from a site that loads the literal 0x510); 0x514 and 0x518 are written by the generic I/O helper FUN_00001bac. The values are literal immediates at real call sites, faithfully recovered from the binary — not inferred. Identification (fw_cfg precedent): 0x510 = fw_cfg SELECTOR (the data port is 0x511), 0x514 = fw_cfg DMA address HIGH, 0x518 = DMA address LOW/trigger — all legitimate QEMU guest/host communication. The allowlist grants the four named ports only (0x510/0x511/0x514/0x518), never a range, and the driver now PASSES. The 9th port (v3.19) comes from the 0x511 PIO-fallback IN path. The 10th port (v3.24) comes from the second 0x511 site (FUN_000045f0, IN at 00004606). The other port findings pass: port 0x0 (legacy DMA range) and CF8/CFC (PCI config range), and the MSR read is SEV_STATUS (0xC0010131, allowlisted). This resolution is about the engine's allowlist, not a claim about the driver — and the old FAIL is kept in this paragraph, not erased.

Provenance

Source: Ubuntu package ovmf-generic 2025.11-3ubuntu7.3, EDK2 2025.11. PE image, 52 functions. No BAR-relative findings: BAR bases arrive at runtime as hardware inputs.

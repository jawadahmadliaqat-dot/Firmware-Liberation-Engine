Recovered in v3.13 — with a correction. The UART TX data path was missing at v3.12, and the original diagnosis (stack spill) was wrong: both TX functions are entered only via direct tail branches, and CALL-only binding gave them no binding site. Slice 4 (direct-branch tail-call sites) recovers all 23. Generic PASS means only that what was found stayed in-window.
